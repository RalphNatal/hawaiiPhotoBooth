It started with one booth at a Christmas service

Hawaii Booths began as a single home-built photo booth for a church Christmas service in Honolulu. It is still run by the people who built it.

How we got here

  1. 1

    A moment of inspiration

    Our church asked for something that would keep people in the hall after the Christmas service instead of heading straight for the car park. We built a booth from a camera, a laptop, a ring light and a curtain rail, and put it up in the corner. By the end of the night there was a queue, and families who had sat next to each other for years were swapping props.

  2. 2

    A passion for memories

    What stayed with us was not the equipment. It was watching people look at their prints afterwards, laughing at the same picture for the third time. A booth does not so much take photographs as give people a reason to stand close together for a moment, and that moment is what they keep.

  3. 3

    From DIY to professional

    The next requests came from weddings and birthdays, and the home-built rig was not going to survive them. We invested in proper camera systems, dye-sublimation printers, a 360 platform and later the Glambot arm, and we learned how to run an event so that the host never has to think about the booth.

  4. 4

    Growing with the community

    Most of our bookings still arrive by word of mouth. A guest at a wedding books us for a company party; someone at that party books us for their daughter's prom. We are based in Honolulu and work across Oʻahu, and we still do the Christmas service.

  5. 5

    Our commitment

    Every package comes with people who set up, run the booth for the full two hours and pack down afterwards. Every photo, video and voicemail is delivered digitally after the event so nothing depends on a print surviving the night. If something goes wrong on the day, we fix it before you notice.
