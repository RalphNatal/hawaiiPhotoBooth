How it works

  1. 1

    Pick up the handset

    The phone sits on a stand with a small sign. A guest lifts the receiver and hears a greeting you have recorded, or one of ours if you would rather not.

  2. 2

    Leave a message

    After the tone they say whatever they like: a toast, a story, a song, a piece of advice. There is no time limit and nobody is listening in. They hang up when they are done.

  3. 3

    Listen afterwards

    Every message is recorded as its own audio file. After the event we send you a link to download all of them, to keep or to share.
